<objective>
Add the three investment-side mutation functions to `backend/writes.py`:
`apply_add_investment_transfer` (liquid→investment, XFER-02), and `apply_add_funded_buy`
/ `apply_add_funded_sell` (funded trade with a cash leg, XFER-03/XFER-04). Closes
SC #2, SC #3, and SC #5 (dual-amount cross-currency legs).

Purpose: Wire liquid cash to investment positions through the single mutation layer
without ever creating a synthetic `accounts` row for investment money (the double-count
bug Phase 12 fixed). Each function composes `apply_add_transaction` +
`apply_add_portfolio_event` and lets `recompute_holding_from_events` own the position
math (D-06). Cross-currency is handled purely by the two rows' own amount/currency
(D-09); no function calls a live-only FX rate (D-10). Never commits (D-01).
Output: `backend/writes.py` gains three functions; plan 01's investment-transfer,
funded-buy one-commit, and dual-currency tests go GREEN.
</objective>
